Act 2 · The solution

One Steering — removing the hassle

MACH — One Agreement

Automated forecasting tracks each agreement's financial performance and commitment variances. Recommendation logic prioritises all group and individual commitments (highest to lowest), then the lowest average IoT outbound rate. Output: downloadable or API-provisioned to steering.

TOMIA — IPN

Receives country-level recommendations and intelligently splits traffic across all subscriber segments (NB-IoT, 5G SA, legacy). Continuously monitors network quality — applies dedicated steering per region or city when conditions deviate from the national norm.

Act 2 · The solution

In practice — three scenarios

Group commitment

Commitment spans multiple country affiliates. One miscalculated affiliate → penalty. One Steering distributes targets correctly across all.

Retail + IoT cross-commitment

Commercial team negotiates a joint retail/IoT commitment. Complex accruals and steering — handled automatically, not on a spreadsheet.

City-level steering · United States

Network A preferred nationwide. IPN detects Network B delivers better coverage in Washington, DC → auto-steers subscribers there without breaking the commercial commitment.

Works at scale, with granularity, in real-world messy environments.
