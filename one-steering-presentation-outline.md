## Segment 2 — The Commercial Problem (1:00 – 3:00 | ~2 min)

**Goal:** Show how the market has become structurally more complex on the commercial side.

- The rise of IoT has fundamentally changed how agreements are designed and managed.
  - Roaming IoT connections projected to grow **300%**, with global cellular IoT revenues rising from $13.8B (2023) to $23.4B by 2028. [[Kaleido Intelligence]](https://kaleidointelligence.com/product/iot-roaming-market-strategies-outlook-2025/)
  - 38% of enterprises require IoT deployments across multiple continents, making traditional roaming models insufficient. [[IoT Now]](https://iot-now.com/2025/11/19/154116-iot-and-mobile-operators-progress-and-pain-points/)
- What was once a single commercial agreement has **multiplied by 4 or 5**.
- Multiple coexisting settlement mechanisms: **TAP, IoT Discount, BCE (Billing & Charging Evolution)** — often running on different billing platforms.
  - TAP was designed for voice and is too rigid for modern IoT/5G use cases (can't handle micro-sessions, lacks threshold-based pricing). [[GSMA – BCE Working Group]](https://www.gsma.com/get-involved/working-groups/interoperability-data-specifications-and-settlement-group/billing-and-charging-evolution)
  - BCE enables flexible settlement schedules and is tailor-made for IoT and 5G wholesale roaming. [[The Fast Mode]](https://www.thefastmode.com/expert-opinion/26566-billing-and-charging-evolution-bce-bringing-out-the-full-value-of-the-5g-iot-roaming) | [[Orange Wholesale]](https://wholesale.orange.com/international/en/knowledge-hub/insights/5g-sa-billing-and-bce-why-wholesale-roaming-charging-between-operators-needs-to-evolve.html)
- Calculations are handled manually → risk of discrepancies, delayed settlements, slower cash flow.
  - Pricing disputes alone can amount to **$2M per MNO**, with resolution taking up to 3 months. [[TOMIA – 5 Roaming Challenges]](https://tomiaglobal.com/insights/5-roaming-challenges-in-5-minutes)

**Key message:** Complexity has outpaced the tools operators currently use.

## Segment 3 — The Network Problem (3:00 – 4:30 | ~1.5 min)

**Goal:** Mirror the commercial problem on the network/engineering side.

- Steering services must replicate every commercial segment into multiple network configurations.
- Segments behave differently: device types vary, access technologies differ, allowed services change, usage patterns diverge.
- Ongoing phase-out of 2G/3G networks forces dynamic, real-time steering.
  - By mid-2025, **278 completed, planned, or in-progress 2G/3G switch-offs** across **83 countries** — 2025 is a peak year (37 2G + 39 3G shut-downs). [[GSA – 2G/3G Switch-Off July 2025]](https://gsacom.com/paper/2g-3g-switch-off-july-2025/) | [[GSMA Intelligence – Network Sunsets Q3 2025]](https://www.gsmaintelligence.com/research/network-sunsets-q3-2025)
  - Regulators such as Ofcom have mapped predicted "2G Inbound Roaming Not-Spots" where legacy roaming will simply stop working. [[Ofcom]](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/coverage-and-speeds/preparing-for-2g-switch-off---devices-using-international-roaming-sims-2g-network-coverage-data-and-predicted-new-2g-inbound-roaming-not-spots)
- Clear correlation: **higher network quality → higher data consumption → more revenue**.
  - 5G users consume up to **2.7× more mobile data** than 4G users in leading markets (Japan, Canada, Germany, South Korea). [[Opensignal – 5G & COVID-19 Mobile Data Report]](https://insights.opensignal.com/reports/2021/06/global-state-of-the-mobile-network)
  - EU roaming tests show customers experience worse quality while roaming vs. at home in **at least 25% of roaming instances** — even when the technical conditions would allow better service. [[European Commission – Roaming Performance Study]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/roaming-performance-study)
- Steering is not just a technical function — it's a **financial lever**.

**Key message:** When commercial intent and network behavior are misaligned, operators lose money. Managing this manually is *"like finding a needle in a haystack."*

## Segment 4 — The Industry Gap (4:30 – 6:00 | ~1.5 min)

**Goal:** Diagnose the current state of the industry and why existing tools fall short.

- Commercial agreements, settlement processes, and steering decisions are managed **in silos**.
  - Operators often rely on multiple billing systems — in some cases still using Excel — to manage different agreement types. [[TOMIA – 5 Roaming Challenges]](https://tomiaglobal.com/insights/5-roaming-challenges-in-5-minutes)
- Most operators lack tools that fully reflect growing complexity across segments, charging models, and network configs.
  - Global telecom operators fail to collect ~**7% of revenue owed** — roughly **$2–2.3 billion annually** — due to billing errors, fraud, and operational inefficiencies. [[Juniper Research – $2B Revenue Leakage]](https://www.juniperresearch.com/whitepapers/mobile-roaming-the-2-bn-revenue-leakage)
- Automation today is **partial**: focused on agreements OR settlement OR steering — never all three.
  - **60–70% of margin-impacting roaming events** occur within the first 30 seconds of network attach — far too fast for human intervention. [[Bankai Infotech – Unified Roaming Suite]](https://www.bankaiinfotech.com/blogs/unified-roaming-revenue-engine/)
  - Operators lose **20–30% of potential wholesale roaming margin** because revenue-side processes remain manual while network signaling is already automated. [[Bankai Infotech]](https://www.bankaiinfotech.com/blogs/unified-roaming-revenue-engine/)
- What's missing: **cross-service automation** that connects all steps and eliminates manual calculations. [[Software Mind – Steering of Roaming Solutions]](https://softwaremind.com/blog/how-network-operators-are-implementing-steering-of-roaming-solutions/)
- The market wants *simplification* — but simplification doesn't mean fewer segments. It means **making complexity manageable and predictable**.

**Key message:** The industry's call for real-time, actionable analytics that close the loop between commercial targets and network execution is unanswered — until now.

## Segment 5 — Introducing One Steering (6:00 – 7:30 | ~1.5 min)

**Goal:** Present the solution clearly and confidently.

- **One Steering** bridges the gap between agreement commitments and real-world subscriber steering.
- Two sides of the solution:
  - **MACH – One Agreement:** Automated forecasting, commitment tracking, steering recommendations with estimated discount cost, downloadable or API-provisioned.
  - **TOMIA – IPN (Intelligent Preferred Network):** Segment-specific steering distributions, granular quality monitoring, region/city-level steering rules.
- Together they create a **closed loop**: commercial intent and network behavior finally work as one.

**Key message:** Neither company could deliver this alone. The power is in the combination.

## Segment 6 — How It Works in Practice (7:30 – 9:00 | ~1.5 min)

**Goal:** Make it concrete with real-world examples.

- Example 1 (Group commitment): An operator has a group-level commitment across multiple country affiliates. If steering for even one affiliate is miscalculated → commitment penalty. One Steering prevents this.
- Example 2 (Cross-commitment): Commercial teams negotiate cross-commitments spanning retail and IoT → complex accruals and steering. One Steering handles this automatically.
- Example 3 (Granular steering): In the US, Network A is preferred nationwide — but IPN detects Network B has better coverage in Washington → automatically steers subscribers to Network B there, without breaking commercial commitments.

**Key message:** The solution works at scale, with granularity, in real-world messy environments.
