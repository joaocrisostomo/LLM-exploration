## Structure at a glance

The presentation is built around a single narrative device: **the closed loop**.

> Commercial agreements → automated recommendations → real-time network execution → back again.

The audience sees this loop on slide 2. Every subsequent section either explains **why it was broken** (Act 1) or **how we fixed it** (Act 2 & 3). This keeps the story tight and the solution front-of-mind throughout.

## Act 1 — The Challenges (1:00 – 5:00 | ~4 min)

### Slide 2 — The Left Side: Commercial Chaos

**Goal:** Show how the commercial side became structurally unmanageable.

- IoT growth turned one agreement per partner into **4–5 agreements**
  - Roaming IoT connections projected to grow **300%**, revenues from $13.8B (2023) to $23.4B by 2028. [[Kaleido Intelligence]](https://kaleidointelligence.com/product/iot-roaming-market-strategies-outlook-2025/)
  - 38% of enterprises need IoT across multiple continents — traditional roaming models don't fit. [[IoT Now]](https://iot-now.com/2025/11/19/154116-iot-and-mobile-operators-progress-and-pain-points/)
- Three settlement mechanisms now coexist: **TAP, IoT Discount, BCE** — often on separate billing platforms
  - **TAP (Transferred Account Procedure):** The industry standard since 1991. Files exchanged monthly, per-event rating, linear charging only. Built for voice — cannot handle IoT micro-sessions, threshold-based pricing, or 5G complexity. [[Syniverse – TAP vs BCE]](https://www.syniverse.com/blog/monetization/exploring-the-key-differences-between-bce-and-tap-proving-out-why-mobile) | [[GSMA – BCE Working Group]](https://www.gsma.com/get-involved/working-groups/interoperability-data-specifications-and-settlement-group/billing-and-charging-evolution)
  - **BCE (Billing & Charging Evolution):** GSMA-standardised successor. Aggregated reports, flexible settlement windows (monthly/quarterly/annual/custom), multi-dimensional charging models. Purpose-built for IoT and 5G. [[The Fast Mode]](https://www.thefastmode.com/expert-opinion/26566-billing-and-charging-evolution-bce-bringing-out-the-full-value-of-the-5g-iot-roaming) | [[Orange Wholesale]](https://wholesale.orange.com/international/en/knowledge-hub/insights/5g-sa-billing-and-bce-why-wholesale-roaming-charging-between-operators-needs-to-evolve.html) | [[Juniper Research]](https://www.juniperresearch.com/resources/blog/how-will-bce-improve-operator-settlement-processes/)
- Calculations handled manually → disputes, delayed settlements, slower cash flow
  - Pricing disputes alone cost **$2M per MNO**, taking up to 3 months to resolve. [[TOMIA – 5 Roaming Challenges]](https://tomiaglobal.com/insights/5-roaming-challenges-in-5-minutes)

**Key message:** Complexity has outpaced the tools operators currently use.

---

### Slide 3 — The Right Side: Network Complexity

**Goal:** Mirror the commercial problem on the engineering/network side.

- Every commercial segment requires its own network configuration; device types, access tech, and usage patterns all diverge
- Ongoing 2G/3G phase-out forces continuous, dynamic steering
  - **278** completed, planned, or in-progress 2G/3G switch-offs across **83 countries** — 2025 is the peak year (37 2G + 39 3G). [[GSA – 2G/3G Switch-Off July 2025]](https://gsacom.com/paper/2g-3g-switch-off-july-2025/) | [[GSMA Intelligence – Network Sunsets Q3 2025]](https://www.gsmaintelligence.com/research/network-sunsets-q3-2025)
  - Ofcom has mapped predicted "2G Inbound Roaming Not-Spots" where legacy roaming will simply stop working. [[Ofcom]](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/coverage-and-speeds/preparing-for-2g-switch-off---devices-using-international-roaming-sims-2g-network-coverage-data-and-predicted-new-2g-inbound-roaming-not-spots)
- Clear financial link: higher network quality → higher data consumption → more revenue
  - 5G users consume up to **2.7× more mobile data** than 4G users in leading markets. [[Opensignal]](https://insights.opensignal.com/reports/2021/06/global-state-of-the-mobile-network)
  - EU tests: **25%+ of roaming instances** deliver suboptimal quality even when better was technically possible. [[European Commission – Roaming Performance Study]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/roaming-performance-study)

**Key message:** Steering is not just a technical function — it's a **financial lever**.

---

### Slide 4 — The Missing Link

**Goal:** Make the gap between the two sides visible and quantifiable.

- Commercial agreements, settlement, and steering managed in **complete silos** — some operators still on Excel
- **$2–2.3 billion** in annual revenue leakage industry-wide from billing errors and operational inefficiencies. [[Juniper Research]](https://www.juniperresearch.com/whitepapers/mobile-roaming-the-2-bn-revenue-leakage)
- **60–70% of margin-impacting roaming events** occur in the first 30 seconds of network attach — no human can react in time. [[Bankai Infotech]](https://www.bankaiinfotech.com/blogs/unified-roaming-revenue-engine/)
- Operators lose **20–30% of potential wholesale roaming margin** because the revenue side stays manual while the network side is already automated. [[Bankai Infotech]](https://www.bankaiinfotech.com/blogs/unified-roaming-revenue-engine/)

**Key message:** The problem isn't complexity — it's that the two sides have never talked to each other.

### Slide 6 — One Steering in Detail

**Goal:** Walk through what each side does.

- Show the same loop diagram, but now fully connected and animated
- **MACH – One Agreement (left side):** Automated forecasting, commitment tracking, steering recommendations with estimated discount cost — downloadable or API-provisioned
- **TOMIA – IPN / Intelligent Preferred Network (right side):** Segment-specific traffic distributions, granular quality monitoring, city/region-level steering rules

**Key message:** Neither company could deliver this alone. The power is in the combination.

---

### Slide 7 — In Practice: Three Scenarios

**Goal:** Make the solution concrete with real-world examples.

- **Example 1 — Group commitment:** A group-level commitment spans multiple country affiliates. If one affiliate is miscalculated → penalty. One Steering distributes targets correctly across all.
- **Example 2 — Retail + IoT cross-commitment:** Commercial team negotiates a joint retail/IoT commitment. Complex accruals and steering — handled automatically.
- **Example 3 — City-level steering (US):** Network A preferred nationwide. IPN detects Network B delivers better coverage in Washington, DC → auto-steers subscribers there without breaking the commercial commitment.

**Key message:** Works at scale, with granularity, in real-world messy environments.

---

### Slide 8 — Technology Stack

**Goal:** Build credibility with technical depth — keep it to 45 seconds.

- **Infrastructure:** AWS S3 for scalability and availability
- **Database layer:** Oracle SQL + Redis + Hadoop for diverse workloads
- **Calculations:** Core processing in SQL; scoring via ML (K-means clustering)
- **Messaging:** ZeroMQ for real-time internal messaging
- **Testing:** COT emulation for full system verification

**Key message:** Built for enterprise scale — speed, reliability, and flexibility.
